# Male chorus samples

Eight sustained notes of the male chorus of the Sonatina Symphonic Orchestra, by Mattias Westlund,
maintained by Peter Eastman at https://github.com/peastman/sso. Each is `Samples/Chorus/chorus-male-{note}.wav`
cut to its steady 6.8 seconds, re-encoded as 64 kbps mp3 at 22.05 kHz and named by its MIDI note: 43 is G2, then every minor third up to 64, E4.
