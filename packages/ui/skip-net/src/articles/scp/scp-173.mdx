<ContainmentProcedures>

```sh title="contain scp-173"
# place it at pick
spawn npc:scp-173 at:$( pick 1 ) as:scp-173

# lock its container's door(s)
lock $( pickGdKey )
```

```sh title="enter its container"
# no fewer than three enter, at picks
pick 3 | spawn npc:d- as:d

# two keep it in view at all times
pose d-0 look:scp-173 &
pose d-1 look:scp-173 &
```

```sh title="keep it in view"
# draw each line of sight, saying why one fails
watch --debug scp-173 with:d-0,d-1,d-2

# who sees it, as that changes
watch scp-173 with:d-0,d-1,d-2

# who sees it now
seen scp-173 by:d-0,d-1,d-2
```

```sh title="blink one at a time"
# d-0 shuts their eyes, whilst d-1 watches
pose d-0 eyes:shut

# d-0 opens them
pose d-0 eyes:open
```

```sh title="leave its container"
# relock the door behind them
lock $( pickGdKey )

# remove personnel
remove d-{0..2}
```

</ContainmentProcedures>

<Description>

SCP-173 is a figure of concrete and rebar, crudely painted.
It is animate, and hostile.

It cannot move whilst it is seen.
So personnel are to blink one at a time, never together.

</Description>
